Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Hatua is a small student project. This page says plainly what it does with your information.

The short version

Your goal, quests, check-ins and hero progress live on your phone. When you send a check-in, the words you typed go to an AI service so it can read them. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Your goal, your quests, your check-ins, your XP and your hero bars.
  • Proof photos and step counts, if you choose to use them. They are read and stored on your phone only.

You can clear everything with Start over in the app menu, or by uninstalling the app.

What leaves your phone

When you make your quests or send a check-in, the words you typed are sent over the internet to a small server function run by the Hatua project. It passes them to an AI provider (Groq) and returns the answer. The Hatua function does not store your words. The AI provider handles them under its own terms. In demo mode, nothing is sent at all.

Photos are never sent. If you use step tracking, only the day's step number may travel with your check-in. Please do not put passwords, private health records or other sensitive details in a check-in.

Purchases

Pro is offered through the app store, and RevenueCat manages subscriptions for Hatua. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase details. It does not receive your goals or check-ins. Payment details are handled by the store and never by Hatua. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Your choices

You decide what you write. You can delete your data at any time from the app menu. If you have a question or want something explained, open an issue at github.com/good-tech3y/hatua.

Age

Hatua is not made for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
